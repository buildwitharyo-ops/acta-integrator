# `/unduh` — lead magnets

Two files are referenced by `components/learn/cta/DownloadCard.tsx` and must be authored by ACTA.
They are **not** generated by tooling and are deliberately absent from this repository until a real
version exists — a placeholder PDF that reaches a procurement officer is worse than no download at all.

| File | What it is | Who wants it |
|---|---|---|
| `acta-template-rks-tata-suara-v1.2.docx` | RKS / tender specification boilerplate for a sound-system scope of work | Specifier & procurement roles — the leg the commercial case stands on |
| `acta-checklist-ruang-rapat-hybrid-v1.0.pdf` | Pre-purchase specification checklist for a hybrid meeting room | Decision-maker role |

## Both are ungated. This is a decision, not an oversight.

Binding decision D9 (`docs/learn-platform/04-ROADMAP-RISKS-AND-BETTER-IDEAS.md`): each is a plain
server-rendered `<a href>` that works with JavaScript disabled and is crawlable. The identity ask sits
*below* the working link, is optional, and asks for a WhatsApp number rather than an email.

Gating would trade roughly 260 monthly impressions and permanent crawlability for roughly 45
unmonitored Gmail addresses. Do not add a form wall, a modal, or an email capture step.

## Why the RKS template matters more than its download count

It is the one asset that can carry ACTA's technical language into a tender document months before the
tender is announced — the standard mechanism by which Indonesian AV projects are actually won, and a
mechanism structurally unavailable to paid search. Its month-15 kill criterion (fewer than 100
cumulative downloads) is a check on the whole specifier strategy, not on the file.

## Filename discipline

The version suffix is part of the URL. When a document is revised, publish a **new** filename and
update `DownloadCard` — never overwrite in place, or every link already pasted into a WhatsApp group
silently starts serving different content than the person who shared it saw.
